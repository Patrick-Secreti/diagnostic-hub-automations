**Honda / Acura — Subscription Purchasing Disruption**
**Industry Reported** · Nov 24, 2026 · Ongoing
**Service** SIS / i-HDS
NASTF confirmed Honda is investigating subscription purchasing issues. As of 8:14 PM ET, SIS purchase buttons were visible, but standard i-HDS purchase buttons remained unavailable.
Full service restoration has not been confirmed.
